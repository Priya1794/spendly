Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name and email address when you create an account, and the expense records you log — including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information, such as browser type and access times, to help keep the Service running smoothly.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide to operate Spendly: displaying your expense history, generating category breakdowns and summaries, and maintaining your account. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and retained for as long as your account remains active. We take reasonable measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party providers for infrastructure, such as hosting or email delivery, to operate the Service. These providers only receive the minimum information necessary to perform their function and are not permitted to use your data for any other purpose. We do not share your expense data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website. We're happy to help.
